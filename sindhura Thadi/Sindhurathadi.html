⏸️

The Legal Lens started, then paused

The Bar Council of India’s July 2026 social media circular reaffirmed Rule 36: no advertising, solicitation or client acquisition online. Many “lawyer reels” now carry real risk.

Video isn’t the comfort zone — yet

Most agencies would push you on camera. We think your voice is the asset. Audio-led content can build authority without a single face-to-camera shoot.

What we will create

  • Legal awareness and “know your rights” explainers
  • Neutral updates on new laws and important judgments
  • Myth-vs-fact posts that correct legal misinformation
  • General answers to common public questions — never case-specific advice
  • Your own recorded voice, with clear, accurate sourcing

What we will never do

  • “Contact me for your case” or any solicitation call-to-action
  • Client testimonials, case wins, fees or success claims
  • Content filmed in or depicting court premises or proceedings
  • AI voice clones, deepfakes or synthetic court/judge imagery
  • Paid ads promoting legal services or influencer endorsements

Guardrails are based on Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules and the BCI circular on social media and digital ethics dated 17 July 2026. We are marketers, not your legal counsel on professional conduct — every post goes to you for final approval, and you remain the final judge of what is appropriate for your practice.
